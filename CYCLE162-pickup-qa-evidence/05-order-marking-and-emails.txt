=== SESSION-LESS FALLBACK (asserted in the suite, not just reasoned about) ===

  WooCommerce copies a shipping RATE's meta onto the order's shipping ITEM, on
  both the classic and the Store API path (WC_Checkout::create_order_shipping_lines(),
  which StoreApi OrderController::update_order_from_cart() calls at line 820 of
  WooCommerce 10.9.1). A pickup order whose session has died therefore still
  carries School and Visit date on the item, and the note is built from those.
  Proven with a probe order that had rate meta and NO session:
    school recovered, date recovered, note still says DO NOT SHIP and names the
    real school.

  If NEITHER a session NOR item meta is available, the note deliberately
  switches to a degraded form that says the detail is MISSING and where to look,
  rather than a blank-filled sentence that reads like an answer. That first
  wording ("at a school visit on the visit date") was produced in QA and was
  replaced for exactly that reason.
