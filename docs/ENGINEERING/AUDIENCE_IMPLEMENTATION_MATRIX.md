## How to read this table

- **Live** — real, functioning, verified in its intended environment (production for customer-facing components, staging for anything not yet approved).
- **Staged** — built and staging-verified, not yet approved/promoted.
- **Draft/inert** — exists as a real object but deliberately non-functional (e.g. a WooCommerce coupon in `draft` status).
- **Not built** — genuinely does not exist yet.
- **N/A by design** — deliberately not applicable to this audience (e.g. no popup needed because the landing page's own panel is the signup mechanism).

## Architecture decision: Mailchimp vs. HubSpot for Retailers/Organizations — resolved 2026-07-15

Retailers and Organizations are relationship/inquiry audiences (bulk-order inquiries, wholesale terms, partnership discussions) rather than pure newsletter-engagement audiences. **Resolved 2026-07-15: Mailchimp owns acquisition and nurture for all five audiences, including Retailers and Organizations.** A future CRM (e.g. HubSpot) may receive qualified inquiries downstream of Mailchimp once one is adopted, but Mailchimp is the confirmed system of record for the acquisition/nurture layer today. Do not re-list this as an open decision.
